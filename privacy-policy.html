4. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

4.1 Digital Purchase Disclosures

Some of our apps and games offer optional digital purchases (such as virtual currencies, cosmetic items, remove-ads upgrades, or other premium content). All such transactions are processed exclusively through the Google Play Billing API. At no point does Mimi Games access, store, or transmit your raw payment credentials (e.g., credit card numbers, bank account details, or Google Pay tokens). Payment processing, authentication, and final charge authorisation are handled entirely by Google in accordance with the Google Payments Privacy Notice and Google Play Terms of Service.

4.2 Data Collected for Purchases

When you initiate or complete an in-app purchase, the following categories of data are generated and processed to fulfil the digital product and prevent fraud:

  • Transaction Details: A Google-issued purchase token, Order ID, product SKU/ID, and purchase timestamp generated by the Google Play Billing API for each successful transaction.
  • Purchase History: A record of which digital products have been purchased on a given installation so that entitlements (e.g., unlocked content, restored purchases) can be granted correctly.
  • Device Parameters: Technical identifiers provided by the Google Play Billing API (such as the device's Android version, Play Store version, and network state at the time of the transaction) used solely to validate receipts and detect anomalous or fraudulent purchase patterns.
  • Acknowledgement & Consumption Status: Whether a consumable product has been consumed or a non-consumable product has been acknowledged, to prevent duplicate grants and accidental refund loops.

Mimi Games does not collect your name, billing address, payment method, or any other personally identifiable financial information as part of this process. All sensitive payment data is processed and stored by Google Play and is governed solely by Google's policies.

4.3 Purpose of Purchase Data

Purchase-related data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Product Fulfilment: To unlock, grant, or restore the digital item(s) the user purchased.
  • Fraud Prevention & Security: To verify purchase authenticity via server-side receipt validation against the Google Play Developer API, detect unusual transaction patterns, and prevent unauthorised access to premium content.
  • Customer Support: To assist users who report missing purchases or request refunds.
  • Legal & Financial Compliance: To satisfy accounting, tax, and consumer-protection legal obligations.

4.4 Retention of Purchase Data

Purchase tokens and associated transaction records are retained for as long as the user's app installation (or linked account, if applicable) remains active. This is necessary to:

  • Continuously validate entitlements and prevent fraudulent re-use of consumed tokens.
  • Enable purchase restoration if the user reinstalls the app or switches devices.
  • Maintain records required by applicable financial and consumer-protection regulations.

Upon uninstalling the app or submitting a verified data-deletion request to developer@mimigames.games, locally cached purchase tokens are erased. Any server-side transaction records are deleted within 30 days of a valid deletion request, except where retention is required by law (e.g., tax records, which are kept for up to 7 years as mandated by applicable regulations).

6. Google Play Games Services (GPGS)

Some of our games integrate Google Play Games Services (GPGS), a social gaming platform operated by Google LLC. When a player signs in to GPGS within our game, certain data is transmitted to Google's infrastructure to power leaderboards, achievements, and cross-device progress saving. This section explains exactly what data is shared, why, and how players can manage or delete it.

6.1 Data Collected & Transmitted to Google Play Games Services

When a player authenticates with Google Play Games Services inside our game, the following data is transmitted to or processed by Google LLC:

  • Google Play Games Player ID: A unique pseudonymous identifier assigned by Google to the player's Google Play Games account. This ID is used to associate leaderboard entries, achievement progress, and game-state data with the correct player profile.
  • Game Progress Metrics: Incremental achievement progress (e.g., step counters toward multi-step achievements), unlocked achievement IDs, and in-game milestone flags are submitted to the GPGS API so that achievement data is synchronised across sessions and devices.
  • High Scores (Leaderboards): A player's best score for each leaderboard-enabled game mode is submitted to Google's leaderboard infrastructure so it can be ranked and displayed publicly (or within a friends list, depending on the player's privacy settings).
  • Game State / Saved Progress: Where cloud-save is enabled, gameplay state (such as completed levels, unlocked items, and settings) may be stored via the GPGS Saved Games API to allow players to resume progress after reinstalling or switching devices.
  • Display Name & Avatar: The player's chosen Google Play Games display name and avatar image (as set in their Google Play Games profile) may be retrieved and shown inside the game's UI for personalisation purposes. Mimi Games does not store this data on its own servers.

This integration is powered by Google LLC (Google Play Services). All data transmitted through GPGS is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Games Services Terms of Service. Mimi Games does not control or have independent access to the underlying personal data held in a player's Google Account.

6.2 Purpose of Processing

Data transmitted to Google Play Games Services is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Social Gaming Features: Displaying the player's high scores on public or friends-only leaderboards, unlocking and syncing achievements (including incremental multi-step achievements), and rewarding Google Play Games profile XP for in-game accomplishments.
  • Cross-Device Progress Saving: Storing incremental game progress so that players retain their achievements, scores, and game state if they switch devices, reinstall the app, or log in on a different Android device.
  • Player Identity & Personalisation: Associating in-game actions with the correct GPGS Player ID to ensure accurate leaderboard ranking and achievement attribution.

Mimi Games does not use GPGS data for advertising targeting, profiling, or any purpose beyond the social gaming features described above.

6.3 User Control & Data Deletion

Players have full control over their Google Play Games profile and the data associated with it. You can manage or delete your data at any time using the following methods:

  • Manage Profile Visibility: Open the Google Play Games app → Profile → Settings to control whether your display name and scores are visible publicly, to friends only, or only to you.
  • Opt Out of Leaderboards: Within the Google Play Games app → Settings, you can disable leaderboard participation so your scores are no longer visible to other players.
  • Delete Google Play Games Profile Data: Open the Google Play Games app → Profile icon → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data. This permanently removes your GPGS profile, leaderboard entries, achievement records, and cloud-saved game data from Google's servers.
  • Sign Out: You can sign out of Google Play Games at any time from within the game's settings menu. After signing out, no further data will be transmitted to GPGS from that session.
  • Google Account Data Request: For a full export or deletion of data held in your Google Account (including GPGS data), visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

If you have concerns about GPGS data that you cannot resolve through the methods above, please contact us at developer@mimigames.games and we will assist where possible.
