About the Game

Modernized Warfare
Reimagined

Xtremeversity is a high-octane, realistic first-person shooter set in a massive, detailed open world. Experience the intensity of modernized warfare on your mobile device like never before.

From tactical urban skirmishes to vast rural battlefields, every corner of Xtremeversity is designed for immersive combat and strategic exploration. Commandeer heavy military vehicles, lead your squad through enemy lines, and master a wide variety of realistic weapons.

Open World Freedom

Explore a vast map filled with tactical targets, hidden enemy outposts, and diverse terrain. From ruined cities to military bases — the world is yours to conquer.

Military Vehicles

Drive and operate realistic military equipment including armored trucks, tactical transports, and high-mobility vehicles designed for modern warfare.

Elite FPS Combat

Engage in realistic first-person combat with advanced ballistics and tactical mechanics. Experience the weight and power of every weapon.

Enemy Soldiers

Encounter intelligent enemy soldiers and rival military factions. Outsmart and outgun heavily armed opponents in dynamic, unscripted encounters.
