Is Rogue Wheel completely free to play?

Yes — Rogue Wheel: Catapult Brawl is 100% free to download and play on Android. No hidden subscriptions or hard paywalls. Download it for free on Google Play.

Can I play Rogue Wheel offline?

Yes! Rogue Wheel is fully offline-capable. No internet connection is required to enjoy the full game — great for travel or areas with no signal.

What makes Rogue Wheel different from other catapult games?

Rogue Wheel combines realistic physics ballistics with rogue-lite progression, meaning every run is different. You unlock new upgrades, face randomized enemy layouts, and must adapt your strategy each time.

Who developed Rogue Wheel?

Rogue Wheel was developed by Mimi Games, an independent mobile game studio based in Sargodha, Punjab, Pakistan. Built with Unity, it is their debut Android title.

What is Puzzle Verse: Mini Game Hub?

Puzzle Verse is a curated collection of bite-sized puzzle games by Mimi Games. Challenge your brain with mini games every day — 100% free on Android.
