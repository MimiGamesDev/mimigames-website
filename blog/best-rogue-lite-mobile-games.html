Rogue-lite games have exploded in popularity on mobile — and for good reason. Whether you have 5 minutes or 5 hours, a good rogue-lite adjusts to you.

Here are our picks for the 5 best rogue-lite mobile games on Android in 2026, ranging from completely free to premium.

01

Rogue Wheel: Catapult Brawl FREE

Developer: Mimi Games (Pakistan)  |  Platform: Android

Rogue Wheel is a unique entry in the rogue-lite genre — instead of a dungeon crawler or action RPG, it is a physics-based catapult game. You launch projectiles with real ballistics at randomized enemy formations, collect mid-run power-ups, and upgrade your catapult permanently between runs. Developed by a Pakistan-based indie game studio, it proves that mobile-first innovation can compete with global titles.

What makes it stand out: the feel of real physics combined with the satisfying destruction chain-reactions. Every run is short, intense, and different. Best of all, it's completely free and works offline — ideal for commutes or travel.

  • ✅ 100% free, no paywalls

  • ✅ Real physics ballistics

Soul Knight is one of the most popular free roguelikes on Android. It features top-down shooter gameplay with hundreds of weapons and characters. While older, it remains one of the best free options because of its depth, co-op, and constant updates.

05

For players who want deep, traditional roguelike gameplay, Shattered Pixel Dungeon is the best free option. Turn-based dungeon crawling with permadeath, incredible depth, and active development. It's not flashy, but it is genuinely challenging and rewarding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free rogue-lite mobile game in 2026?

Rogue Wheel: Catapult Brawl and Soul Knight are the top free options. Rogue Wheel offers unique physics-based catapult gameplay with permanent progression, zero paywalls, and full offline support. Soul Knight delivers deep top-down shooter action with hundreds of weapons and local co-op.

What is the difference between roguelike and rogue-lite?

Roguelikes traditionally feature permadeath with no persistent upgrades — every run starts from scratch. Rogue-lites (like Rogue Wheel and Dead Cells) carry some progression between runs, such as permanent upgrades, unlocked characters, or currency that persists. Most mobile games in the genre are rogue-lites because the meta-progression loop suits shorter mobile sessions.

Are there any good offline rogue-lite games for Android?

Yes. Rogue Wheel: Catapult Brawl, Shattered Pixel Dungeon, and Soul Knight all work fully offline with no internet connection required. These are ideal for travel, commutes, or areas with limited connectivity.

Which rogue-lite mobile games have no energy system or pay-to-win?

Rogue Wheel: Catapult Brawl and Shattered Pixel Dungeon have no energy bars, stamina systems, or pay-to-win mechanics. Dead Cells is a paid premium game with no microtransactions. These titles respect your time and let you play as much as you want.
