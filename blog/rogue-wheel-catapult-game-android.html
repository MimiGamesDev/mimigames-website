Rogue Wheel: Catapult Brawl is a free Android game developed by Mimi Games — a mobile game studio based in Sargodha, Punjab, Pakistan. It combines real physics-based catapult mechanics with rogue-lite progression to create a uniquely satisfying destruction game.

This guide covers everything: how to download it, how to play, the best upgrade strategies, and tips to get the longest runs.

What Is Rogue Wheel?

Rogue Wheel is a free catapult physics game for Android that sits at the intersection of two popular genres: physics-based destruction games (think Angry Birds, but with real ballistics) and rogue-lite progression (like Hades or Dead Cells, but designed for one-handed mobile play).

In Rogue Wheel, you control a catapult and launch projectiles at waves of enemies and structures. Every run is different — enemy formations are randomized, power-ups are procedurally generated, and your upgrade path changes each session.

When you die, you lose progress for that run, but you keep coins to permanently upgrade your catapult for future runs.

💡 Key fact: Rogue Wheel is 100% free to download and works fully offline. No internet connection required.

Gameplay Basics: How to Play

The core gameplay loop in Rogue Wheel is simple to learn but deep to master:

1. Aim Your Catapult

Tap and drag on screen to set your launch angle and power. A trajectory arc shows where your projectile will travel. The physics engine is realistic — wind and gravity affect your shot, so you need to aim slightly higher for longer distances.

2. Destroy Enemies and Structures

Launch projectiles at enemy formations. Hitting structural weak points causes chain-reaction collapses — these are worth bonus damage. Prioritize stacked formations for maximum destruction per shot.

3. Collect Power-Ups Mid-Run

During each run, you'll find power-up drops that temporarily enhance your catapult: larger projectiles, explosive ammo, rapid fire, and more. Choose wisely — some synergize better with your current build.

4. Survive as Long as Possible

Enemies get stronger every wave. Your goal is to last as many waves as possible before your catapult is overwhelmed. Every run ends with coins you can spend on permanent upgrades.

Best Upgrade Strategy

Between runs, you can spend earned coins on permanent upgrades in the catapult workshop. Here's the priority order for beginners:

  • Projectile Power (Priority 1) — More damage per shot means faster enemy kills, reducing the threat sooner
  • Reload Speed (Priority 2) — A faster catapult means more shots per wave
  • Ammo Capacity (Priority 3) — More shots before reloading is crucial for long waves
  • Catapult Armor (Priority 4) — Survivability becomes key in later waves
  • Special Ammo Unlock (Priority 5) — Explosive and chain-shot ammo are game-changing for high waves

💡 Pro Tip: In the early game, always prioritize Projectile Power over Reload Speed. One powerful shot beats three weak ones — enemy health scales faster than your fire rate advantage.

Booster Tips and Tricks

Rogue Wheel features several booster abilities you unlock as you progress:

  • Fireball: Launches a flaming projectile that sets a zone on fire. Best used on clustered enemies
  • Iron Blade: A penetrating shot that passes through multiple enemies in a line
  • Explosive Barrel: Lob a barrel that detonates on impact — massive AOE damage
  • Flame Thrower: Short-range but continuous — best for slowing incoming hordes
  • Health Boost: Save this for emergencies, not offensive purposes

Why Rogue Wheel Stands Out Among Catapult Games

There are many catapult and physics games on Android. What makes Rogue Wheel different?

  • Real ballistic physics — Not simplified arcade arcs, but genuine physics simulation
  • Rogue-lite depth — Permanent progression means every run improves your future runs
  • Offline play — Works without internet, great for travel
  • One-handed controls — Designed specifically for mobile, no dual-stick complexity
  • Made in Pakistan — Indie game from a small passionate team in Sargodha, Punjab
