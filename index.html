We Build Games

"Where Fantasy Becomes Playable — one mobile game at a time."

Mimi Games is based in Sargodha, Punjab, Pakistan. From our first Unity project we have been dedicated to pushing the boundaries of mobile gaming — crafting polished, immersive experiences that resonate with players worldwide.

We believe every game should not only entertain but also leave a lasting impression. Our team fuses smooth gameplay, beautiful visuals, and thoughtful systems — whether that's an intense FPS or a relaxing puzzle adventure.

Performance is our religion. Every title we ship is engineered to run buttery-smooth across all Android devices, from flagships to budget phones. We represent Pakistan on the global gaming stage — and we're just getting started.

Getting Started

How to Play
Rogue Wheel

Rogue Wheel: Catapult Thrower is a free catapult physics game for Android that combines rogue-lite progression with satisfying destruction mechanics. Here's how to get started:

01
Download Free on Google Play

Search "Rogue Wheel" on Google Play Store or visit our page. The game is 100% free to install — no credit card needed.

02
Aim Your Catapult

Tap and drag to set your launch angle and power. Real physics ballistics means every shot behaves differently based on your aim.

03
Destroy Enemies & Structures

Launch projectiles at enemy formations and fortifications. Chain-reactions and collapse physics reward precision shots with bonus damage.

04
Unlock Upgrades Between Runs

After each rogue run, spend earned coins to upgrade your catapult's power, ammo type, and special abilities — then try again with a stronger loadout.

"A surprisingly deep catapult game — each run feels different thanks to the randomized enemy layouts and upgrade paths."

Rogue Wheel is perfect for anyone who loves physics puzzle games, destruction simulators, or rogue-lite mobile games. The one-handed control scheme makes it ideal for casual play sessions as well as intense high-score runs.

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Q
Is Rogue Wheel completely free to play?

Yes — Rogue Wheel: Catapult Thrower is 100% free to download and play on Android. No hidden subscriptions or hard paywalls. Download it for free on Google Play.

Q
Can I play Rogue Wheel offline?

Yes! Rogue Wheel is fully offline-capable. No internet connection is required to enjoy the full game — great for travel or areas with no signal.

Q
What makes Rogue Wheel different from other catapult games?

Rogue Wheel combines realistic physics ballistics with rogue-lite progression, meaning every run is different. You unlock new upgrades, face randomized enemy layouts, and must adapt your strategy each time — unlike most simple catapult games.

Q
Who developed Rogue Wheel?

Rogue Wheel was developed by Mimi Games, an independent mobile game studio based in Sargodha, Punjab, Pakistan. Built with Unity, it is their debut Android title.

Q
Is Xtremeversity coming to Android?

Yes! Xtremeversity — an open-world FPS mobile game — is currently in development by Mimi Games and will be released on Android. Follow us on YouTube for updates.
