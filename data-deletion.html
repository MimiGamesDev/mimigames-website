Policy Overview

Mimi Games (developed by Muhammad Ahsen) is committed to your privacy. This page explains exactly what data categories exist, how each can be deleted or managed, and how long each is retained. It covers four distinct data domains: Advertising & Analytics, In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing), Google Play Games Services (Leaderboards & Achievements), and Technical Diagnostics.

Where Mimi Games holds data on its own systems, we will respond to verified deletion requests within 30 days. Where data is held exclusively by a third party (Google, Unity, etc.), we provide the direct self-service path to delete it.

1. Advertising ID & Ad-Partner Data

Data collected by our advertising partners (AdMob, Unity LevelPlay, InMobi, etc.) is linked to your device's Advertising ID (AAID). You can delete this at any time:

Android
  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Go to Google > Ads.
  3. Tap "Delete advertising ID" — takes effect immediately.

2. Analytics & Activity Data

Activity data (session length, levels completed, events) collected by Firebase Analytics and Unity Analytics is used anonymously to improve gameplay. To stop collection and delete associated identifiers:

  1. Go to Settings > Apps > [Game Name].
  2. Tap Storage > Clear Cache and Clear Data.
  3. Alternatively, reset your Advertising ID (see Section 1 above).

3. Technical Diagnostics

Crash logs and app-performance data are collected anonymously and are not linked to any specific user identity. Because of this, they cannot be manually deleted but are automatically purged by our service providers according to their standard retention schedules (typically 90 days).

4. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

When you make an in-app purchase, the Google Play Billing API generates transaction records — including a purchase token, Order ID, product SKU, and timestamp. Mimi Games receives only this anonymised confirmation data; your payment credentials are held exclusively by Google.

Restore Purchases

Non-consumable entitlements are automatically restored when you sign in with the same Google account on any device via Google Play Billing — no manual action required.

Cancel a Subscription & Stop Auto-Renewal

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select the relevant game subscription and tap Cancel subscription.
  4. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period — no further charges will be made.

Request a Refund

Refunds are processed by Google in accordance with the Google Play refund policy. To request one, visit play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory, locate the order, and select Request a refund. For additional help, email us at developer@mimigames.games.

Delete Purchase Records Held by Mimi Games

Purchase tokens and entitlement records that Mimi Games holds are retained for as long as your installation is active (for entitlement validation) and up to 7 years for tax / accounting records as required by law. To request deletion of all non-legally-required purchase records:

  1. Email developer@mimigames.games with the subject line "IAP Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your Google Play Order ID(s) or approximate purchase date(s) to help us locate your records.
  3. We will confirm deletion within 30 days. Note that financial records legally required for tax purposes (up to 7 years) cannot be deleted before that period expires.

Your Google-side order history can be reviewed at myaccount.google.com/payments-and-subscriptions.

5. Google Play Games Services (Leaderboards & Achievements)

When you sign in to Google Play Games Services (GPGS) in our games, your GPGS Player ID, high scores, achievement progress, and cloud-saved game state are stored by Google. Mimi Games does not independently hold this data; it exists within your Google account.

Manage Profile Visibility

Open the Google Play Games app → tap your profile icon → Settings → adjust who can see your display name and scores (Public / Friends / Only you).

Opt Out of Leaderboards

In the Google Play Games app → Settings, you can disable leaderboard participation so your scores are no longer visible to other players.

Sign Out of Google Play Games

You can sign out of GPGS from within each game's settings menu. After signing out, no further data will be transmitted to Google Play Games from that session.

Permanently Delete Your Google Play Games Profile

Permanent — Cannot Be Undone
  1. Open the Google Play Games app.
  2. Tap your profile icon → Settings.
  3. Tap "Delete Play Games account & data".
  4. Confirm deletion. This permanently removes your GPGS profile, all leaderboard scores, achievement records, and cloud-saved game data from Google's servers.

For a full export or deletion of data held in your Google Account (including GPGS data), visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Specific Inquiries & Formal Requests

If you have a specific inquiry regarding data handling, or wish to make a formal data deletion request (including for IAP purchase records or GPGS data), please contact us at: developer@mimigames.games.

Please include in your email:

  • The type of data you want deleted (Advertising ID data, IAP records, GPGS data, etc.).
  • Your Google Play Order ID(s) (for IAP requests) or GPGS Player ID (for GPGS requests), if available.
  • The game(s) the request relates to.

We are committed to responding to all legitimate requests within 30 days.
