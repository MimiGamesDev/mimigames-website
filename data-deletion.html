Policy Overview

Mimi Games (developed by Muhammad Ahsen) does not use user accounts or store personal data on our servers. We prioritize your privacy and ensure that our games are designed to be safe and transparent.

How to Delete Your Data

Most data collected by our partners (such as AdMob or InMobi) is linked to your device's Advertising ID. You can delete this data yourself at any time by following these steps:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Go to Google > Ads.
  3. Tap "Delete advertising ID."

Analytics & Activity Data

Activity data (such as game levels completed or items clicked) collected by Firebase and Unity Analytics is used anonymously to improve gameplay. To stop this collection and delete associated activity identifiers, please clear your app cache and data in your Android Settings, or reset your Advertising ID.

Technical Diagnostics

Technical data such as crash logs and app performance diagnostics are collected anonymously to improve game stability. Because this data is not linked to a specific user identity, it cannot be manually deleted; however, it is automatically purged by our service providers (AdMob/Google) according to their standard retention policies (typically 90 days).

Data Deletion Summary

Data Type Deletion Method Retention Period
Personal Data / Accounts None Collected N/A
Advertising ID (AdMob/InMobi) Device Settings > Google > Ads Deleted instantly upon reset
Activity Data (Firebase/Unity) Clear App Cache / Data Until cleared by user
Technical Diagnostics (Crashes) Automatic (Provider Managed) Typically 90 days

Specific Inquiries

If you have a specific inquiry regarding data handling or wish to make a formal data deletion request, please contact us at: developer@mimigames.games.

We are committed to responding to all legitimate requests within 30 days.

This page is intended to comply with Google Play Console Data Safety policies regarding user data deletion requests.
